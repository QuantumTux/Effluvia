# Andromedan Systems

If you've played SFB enough to explore the empires beyond the Feds, Klingons and Romulans, then you know about the **Andromedans**. They break _**all**_ the
rules. Forget shields, they have Power Absorbers; don't shoot them, you'll just make them angry _plus_ give them more power to shoot back. And your transporters
are sooooo cute, beaming six whole people at once! Yeah, well, the Andros can beam **entire ships**, like the one that just materialized directly behind you. It's
frightening to imagine what other technology they have, isn't it?
