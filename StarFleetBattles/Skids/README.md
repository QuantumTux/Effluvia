# Skids
Skids were introduced in **Module R8** as enhancements to pods carried by freighters (and tugs). They won't turn a Large Freighter into a dreadnought, but they 
can help defend it, or even make the crew some (probably legal) cash on the side.

## SFB-Rule-MedicalSkid-By-DavidBank.pdf
New colonies rarely had a lot of doctors, and needed frequent stops by independent freighters. This skid enables a freighter to provide medical services and not 
merely haul cargo. Just trust what the medical computer says, and don't ask to see the crew's medical credentials.

## SFB-Rule-PointDefenseSkid-By-DavidBank.pdf
For freighter captains, calling the cops wasn't always an option. This skid adds defensive capability to help a ship fend off attacks.

## SFB-Rule-PriorityDeliverySkid-By-DavidBank.pdf
Long-time SFB players will remember the **Federation Express** delivery ships. But even the USPS doesn't deliver _all_ the US Mail; some of that gets hired out.

### SFB-Rule-RepairSkid-By-DavidBank.pdf
Frontier planets and less-developed colony worlds often needed to repair ships that could not land on the planet. A Repair Skid let a freighter captain bring 
orbital repair services to those places.
### SFB-Rule-SpecialityCargoSkid-By-DavidBank.pdf
Your average cargo container can't handle **Tholian Gem Flowers**. What is the enterprising freighter captain to do?
